Aftercare & questions

Good to know

How long does a lash lift last?

Usually around 6 to 8 weeks, as your natural lashes grow and shed. Brow lamination usually lasts around 4 to 6 weeks.

What's the difference between a lash lift and a Korean lash lift?

Both curl your natural lashes. A Korean lash lift focuses the lift right at the root using a gentler method, giving a softer, fanned, wide-eyed look. Not sure which suits you? Book a free consultation.

Lash lift aftercare
  • Keep lashes dry for 24 hours: no steam, saunas, swimming or hot showers on the face.
  • No mascara for 24 hours.
  • Avoid oil-based make-up removers around the eyes.
  • Try not to sleep face-down on the first night.
Brow lamination aftercare
  • Keep brows dry for 24 hours and avoid brow make-up.
  • Brush them into place each morning.
  • After 24 hours, a little nourishing brow oil helps keep them healthy.
Why do I need a patch test?

Lifting, lamination and tinting products can cause a reaction in a small number of people. A quick patch test at least 48 hours before your first treatment keeps you safe. It's free.

What if I need to cancel?

Please give at least 24 hours' notice so the time can be offered to someone else. You can manage your booking from your confirmation email.
